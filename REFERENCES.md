## Source-use and interpretation notes

-   The source report states that the combined figures draw on multiple
    providers and that ranges or more conservative figures are used
    where providers disagree.
-   Public ransomware leak-site claims should not be treated as
    confirmed incidents. They may include duplicates, outdated entries
    or inflated claims and may omit victims that are not publicly
    disclosed.
-   The India-specific sector exposure ratings are analyst assessments
    because the report did not identify a published dataset providing a
    complete India-specific sector breakdown.
-   The ATT&CK mappings in the report are analyst mappings. Validate
    technique and sub-technique selections against the current ATT&CK
    release before using them to build detections.
-   Access date stated in the source report: **19 November 2026**.
